Chapter IV

A Letter, Sealed With Love

My dearest Riddhu,

I owe you a birthday wish — and more than that, I owe you the truth: you were never forgotten for a single hour. Papa was carrying a mountain that day; even mountains are carried for daughters like you.

You are fourteen now — old enough to read between my lines, young enough to still hold my finger while crossing life's busiest roads. Keep holding it a little longer; the roads are worth it when I walk them with you.

They measure kings by crowns and palaces. I measure my kingdom differently — in your laughter at the dinner table, in your courage on riverbanks and mountain trails, in the young woman you are becoming: strong, kind, unshakeable.

So here is your wish, late by a day but true for a lifetime: Happy Birthday, my Princess. Whatever you dream, I will cross storms to fetch it. Wherever you go, my blessing walks ahead of you. Whenever you fall, my hand reaches before the ground does.

Papa

Mahesh Koria — Your First Hero, Your Forever Fan
